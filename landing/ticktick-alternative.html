An open-source TickTick alternative

If you came to TickTick for a real GTD system and kept running into the list cap, the task cap, or a filter you can’t save without Premium, Mindwtr is worth a look. It is completely free — no subscription, no premium tier, no trial that ends — open source under AGPL-3.0, and it never asks you to create an account. Your tasks live in a local database on your own device, and sync happens only where you point it. TickTick is a good product; this page is about the parts of it that make people leave.

What you gain by switching

The caps go first. Mindwtr has no list limit, no task limit, no checklist limit, and no reminder limit, because there is nothing to upsell: it is free software under AGPL-3.0, funded by donations. Saved searches, calendar views, and the board are part of the app. You also stop assembling the method yourself. Mindwtr ships GTD as structure — Inbox processing, Next, Waiting and Someday, Areas, sequential projects that surface only the task you can act on, and a guided Weekly Review. And there is no account: no sign-up, no email verification, and no server-side copy of your tasks unless you configure a sync target yourself. Your data sits in a local SQLite database with JSON export built in.

Local-first, still on all your devices

Leaving a cloud task manager usually means giving up sync. It doesn’t here. Mindwtr is offline by default — that is the normal state, not a degraded one — and multi-device when you want it: choose iCloud, Dropbox, WebDAV, a folder watched by Syncthing, or a small server you host yourself, and every device merges changes locally with a documented conflict-resolution algorithm. File-based targets can carry an optional passphrase that encrypts the files before they leave your device, so your sync provider stores ciphertext. AI features work the same way: bring your own API key or run an on-device model, instead of sending tasks to a vendor’s service. Native apps cover Linux, macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android.

What TickTick does better

TickTick is a broader product, and some of that breadth is real. It bundles a habit tracker, an Eisenhower matrix, and countdowns that Mindwtr has no equivalent for. It is built for more than one person: you can share a list, assign a task, and comment on it. Mindwtr is a single-person tool with no shared workspaces, assignment, or team threads. TickTick’s hosted integrations — Google Calendar, browser and email plugins, a Telegram bot, third-party connectors — reach further than Mindwtr’s local API, CLI, and MCP automation. It also offers a timeline view, location reminders on iOS, and 40-plus themes. If those are the features holding your system together, TickTick is the better fit, and that is a fine answer.

Moving your TickTick data across

It is one import, and it takes about thirty seconds. In TickTick’s web app, open your avatar → Settings → Account → Backup & Import → Generate Backup and save the file. In Mindwtr, open Settings → Data → Import from TickTick, choose that CSV or ZIP, check the preview summary, and confirm. Folders become Areas, lists become projects, tags stay tags, and priorities, due and start dates, time zones, recurrence, checklists, notes, and completed status come across where the backup records them. Mindwtr saves a recovery snapshot of your existing data first and reports anything it could not map. The full walkthrough, including what backups don’t carry, is in the TickTick import guide.

Common questions

Is Mindwtr really free, or is this a free tier?

Really free. Mindwtr is free software under the AGPL-3.0 license — there is no premium plan, no trial period, no in-app purchase, and no feature behind a paywall. Development is funded by donations.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for and no email to verify. You install the app and start capturing; the app works fully without ever contacting a server.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in a local SQLite database, with JSON export and backup you control. See data & sync for the on-disk format and backup guidance.

Will sync work across my phone and laptop?

Yes, once you pick a route: iCloud, Dropbox, WebDAV, a synced folder, or your own small server. Sync is opt-in — turn it off and Mindwtr is a purely on-device app.

Can I leave again if it isn’t for me?

Any time. Your data is already on your disk in a documented format, and CSV/JSON export is built in. No export fee, no account to close, nothing to cancel.
