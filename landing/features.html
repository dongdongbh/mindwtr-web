From an idea to a finished task

Capture what is on your mind, decide the next step, and keep your lists up to date. New to GTD? Start here →

  • Capture →

    Quick add from anywhere — global hotkey, tray, share sheet, or voice.

  • Clarify →

    Guided inbox processing with the two-minute rule.

  • Organize →

    Projects, sections, contexts, and status lists.

  • Reflect →

    Daily and weekly review workflows with reminders.

  • Engage →

    Context-filtered next actions, so you always know what’s next.

See the tasks you need

Switch between your next actions, projects, calendar, and board.

  • Focus →

    A time-based agenda and your next actions, together in one screen.

  • Projects →

    Multi-step outcomes with sections, areas, and manual task ordering.

  • Calendar →

    Plan dated work and subscribe to external calendars and ICS feeds.

  • Board →

    Kanban-style drag and drop across Inbox, Next, Waiting, and Someday.

  • Contexts & tags →

    Tag tasks by where you do them, like @home or @work/meetings; nested contexts match their parent.

  • Review →

    Daily and weekly review wizards keep the system trustworthy.

Tools for everyday tasks

Find a note, record a thought, repeat a routine, or start a focus timer.

  • Global search →

    Find tasks, notes, and projects. Add filters when you need a narrower search.

  • Audio capture →

    Speak a task; it’s transcribed and filed into your inbox automatically.

  • Pomodoro Focus →

    Alternate focused work and breaks with an optional timer.

  • Reusable lists →

    Duplicate tasks or reset checklists for routines you run again and again.

  • Attachments & notes →

    Files, links, and Markdown notes with preview on any task.

  • Fluid recurrence →

    The next date is calculated after you finish, not on a fixed schedule.

Keep control of your data

Import your tasks, keep backups, and choose whether and where to sync.

  • On your device →

    Tasks, notes, projects, and attachments start on your device (local-first).

  • Flexible sync →

    Choose iCloud, Dropbox, a folder, WebDAV, or your own sync server.

  • Import →

    Bring in your tasks from Todoist, TickTick, DGT GTD, or OmniFocus.

  • Obsidian →

    Import a vault with deep links back to the source notes (desktop).

  • External calendars →

    Read system and Apple calendars, push dated tasks, and subscribe to ICS.

  • Backup & restore →

    Export to JSON and restore from a validated Mindwtr backup.

Connect other tools when you need them

Use Shortcuts, AI, or scripts to work with your tasks. All are optional.

  • AI assistant →

    Use your own provider API credentials or a local model.

  • Local REST API →

    A token-authenticated localhost API for scripting the desktop app.

  • MCP server →

    Let compatible AI tools read and manage your tasks through Mindwtr’s MCP server.

  • Apple Shortcuts & Siri →

    Automate capture and actions on Apple devices.

  • Web app (PWA) →

    Browser access with offline support.

  • Docker →

    Run the PWA and the self-hosted sync server with Docker.

On your computer, phone, and watch

Capture, plan, and review on desktop and mobile. Choose the app for your device below.

  • Desktop →

    macOS, Windows, and Linux.

  • Mobile →

    iOS and Android, with the full guided workflow plus share-sheet, voice, and widget capture.

  • Apple Watch →

    Capture Inbox items by voice or text, act on Focus tasks, and control Pomodoro from your wrist. Included starting with the next stable release.

  • Keyboard →

    Vim and Emacs keymap presets for keyboard-driven work.

  • Themes →

    Light and dark, system-aware.

  • 20 language options →

    Choose your language in Settings. See the full list in the docs.
